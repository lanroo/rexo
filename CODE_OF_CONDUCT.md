# Code of conduct

We want REXO to be a constructive, safe, and technically honest community.

Be respectful. Critique ideas and evidence rather than people. Do not harass,
threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or use sexualized conduct.
Do not misrepresent experimental functionality as production-ready.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior undermines community safety. Serious incidents should be reported
privately through the repository's security or maintainer contact channels.
